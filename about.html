About the chapter

A student body built on the shield of the profession.

NADSAN-FUGUS carries the caduceus and the harvest — medicine and food — as one mission: preparing Nutrition and Dietetics students at FUGUS for the profession ahead.

Who we are

NADSAN-FUGUS is the Federal University Gusau chapter of the Nutrition and Dietetics Students Association of Nigeria (NADSAN) — the recognized national student body for the discipline. As a chapter, we operate under our own elected executive council and a constitution written specifically for the FUGUS student body.

Our members are students in the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, working together on academics, professional exposure, and welfare throughout their time at FUGUS.

What we stand for

Mission

To advance the academic, professional, and welfare interests of Nutrition and Dietetics students at Federal University Gusau.

Vision

A chapter where every student graduates informed, connected, and ready for practice in nutrition and dietetics.

Values

Academic excellence, professional integrity, and service to fellow students — the same standards the national body upholds.
