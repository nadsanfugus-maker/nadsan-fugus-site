Chapter Constitution

Nutrition and Dietetics Students' Association of Nigeria — Federal University Gusau (FUGUS) Chapter. Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, Federal University Gusau, Zamfara State, Nigeria.

Preamble

We, the students of the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, Federal University Gusau (FUGUS), in our collective desire to promote academic excellence, professional development, unity, and the noble practice of nutrition and dietetics science, hereby establish the NADSAN FUGUS Chapter.

Believing firmly that nutrition is the bedrock of human health and national development, and that a strong, vibrant, and well-organised student body is essential to the advancement of our noble profession, we resolve to govern ourselves by this Constitution — in fairness, transparency, and service to our members, our department, and our nation.

Article I

Name, Identity, and Affiliation

  1. The name of this association shall be the Nutrition and Dietetics Students' Association of Nigeria, Federal University Gusau Chapter, hereinafter referred to as "NADSAN FUGUS Chapter" or simply "the Association".
  2. The Association is the official student body of nutrition and dietetics students within the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, Federal University Gusau (FUGUS).
  3. The Association is an officially recognised chapter of the national body — Nutrition and Dietetics Students' Association of Nigeria (NADSAN) — and shall at all times operate in accordance with the national constitution and policies, except where this Constitution makes specific provisions applicable at the chapter level.
  4. The Association shall have a seal, flag, and/or colours as approved by its Congress, reflecting the green and gold identity of the nutrition and dietetics profession in Nigeria.

Article II

Vision, Mission, and Core Values

Section 1 — Vision

To be the most vibrant, impactful, and professionally distinguished chapter of NADSAN in Nigeria, producing competent, ethical, and compassionate nutrition and dietetics professionals.

Section 2 — Mission

To promote academic excellence, professional development, community health education, and the welfare of nutrition and dietetics students at Federal University Gusau through structured programmes, collaborative leadership, and active engagement with the nutrition profession.

Section 3 — Core Values

The Association shall be guided by the following core values:

  • Excellence: Commitment to the highest academic and professional standards.
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty, fairness, and transparency in all dealings.
  • Service: Dedicating time and talent to the betterment of members and the community.
  • Unity: Fostering brotherhood and sisterhood among all members regardless of background.
  • Innovation: Embracing new ideas and approaches in nutrition science and student engagement.

Article III

Aims and Objectives

The aims and objectives of the Association shall be:

  1. To unite all students of nutrition and dietetics at Federal University Gusau under one body for mutual academic and professional benefit.
  2. To promote and maintain high standards of academic performance, professional ethics, and discipline among members.
  3. To create platforms for the exchange of knowledge, skills, and experiences through seminars, workshops, symposia, tutorials, and academic competitions.
  4. To serve as a link between students, the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, the University, and the wider nutrition profession in Nigeria.
  5. To organise and participate in community nutrition education, outreach programmes, and public health campaigns.
  6. To protect and advance the welfare, rights, and interests of all members.
  7. To foster a spirit of friendship, solidarity, and professional pride among nutrition and dietetics students.
  8. To collaborate with NADSAN national body, the Nigerian Institute of Food Science and Technology (NIFST), the Association of Dietitians of Nigeria (ADON), and other relevant professional organisations.
  9. To engage in fundraising and resource mobilisation for the development of the Association and its members.
  10. To celebrate and promote the cultural and professional heritage of nutrition and dietetics in Nigeria.

Article IV

Membership

Section 1 — Categories of Membership

Membership of the Association shall be open to all registered students of the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, Federal University Gusau. There shall be three categories of membership:

  • Ordinary Members: All students who are duly registered and enrolled in the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, FUGUS, shall automatically be ordinary members of the Association upon payment of the prescribed dues.
  • Associate Members: Graduates and alumni of the department who wish to maintain a voluntary affiliation with the Association may be admitted as associate members. Associate members shall not hold office or vote in elections.
  • Honorary Members: Distinguished individuals who have rendered exceptional service to the Association or to the nutrition profession may be conferred honorary membership by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Congress.

Section 2 — Rights of Ordinary Members

Every ordinary member in good standing shall be entitled to:

  1. Vote in all elections and referenda of the Association.
  2. Contest and hold office in the Association, subject to eligibility requirements.
  3. Participate in all programmes, events, and activities organised by the Association.
  4. Access information about the activities and finances of the Association.
  5. Seek redress through established channels for any grievance arising within the Association.

Section 3 — Duties of Members

Every member shall:

  1. Pay all prescribed dues and levies promptly.
  2. Attend meetings and programmes of the Association regularly.
  3. Uphold and defend the Constitution and decisions of the Association.
  4. Maintain decorum, respect, and professional conduct in all Association activities.
  5. Promote the image and interests of the Association at all times.

Section 4 — Suspension and Expulsion

  1. A member may be suspended or expelled for gross misconduct, dishonesty, persistent non-compliance with the Constitution, or actions that bring the Association into disrepute.
  2. No member shall be suspended or expelled without due investigation and an opportunity to be heard before the Disciplinary Committee.
  3. Expulsion shall require a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Congress and shall be subject to appeal.

Article V

Governance Structure

The Association shall be governed through the following organs: the Congress (General Assembly), the Executive Council, the Senate (Council of Class Representatives), and Standing Committees.

Section 1 — The Congress (General Assembly)

  1. The Congress shall be the supreme organ of the Association, consisting of all ordinary members.
  2. The Congress shall meet in ordinary session at least once per semester and may be convened in extraordinary session upon request by the President, the Executive Council, or a petition signed by at least one-third of the membership.
  3. Quorum for a Congress meeting shall be one-half (50%) of the registered membership.
  4. The Congress shall have the power to: ratify or amend the Constitution; elect officers; receive and adopt reports and accounts of the Executive Council; approve the annual budget; impose or remove levies on members; and impeach officers in accordance with due process.

Section 2 — The Executive Council

The Executive Council is the principal executive organ of the Association, responsible for day-to-day management and implementation of Congress decisions. It shall consist of:

  • President
  • Vice President
  • Secretary General
  • Assistant Secretary General
  • Financial Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Director of Academics
  • Director of Socials and Welfare
  • Director of Public Relations
  • Director of Sports and Recreation
  • Tutorial Coordinator
  • Social Media and IT Officer
  • Ex-Officio Members (immediate past President and Vice President)

The Executive Council shall meet at least once every month during the academic session. Decisions are by simple majority; the President holds the casting vote in the event of a tie.

Section 3 — The Senate (Council of Class Representatives)

The Senate consists of one duly elected Class Representative from each level (100, 200, 300, and 400 Level), serving as a consultative and advisory body to the Executive Council. It meets at least once per semester and may submit recommendations to the Executive Council and Congress.

Article VI

Duties of Executive Officers

The President

Chief executive and spokesperson of the Association; presides over all Executive Council and Congress meetings; represents the Association externally; ensures implementation of Congress and Executive Council decisions; submits an annual report to the Congress.

The Vice President

Assists the President in all official duties and acts as President in their absence; supervises the activities of Standing Committees; performs other duties assigned by the President.

The Secretary General

Chief administrative officer; keeps minutes of all meetings and maintains official correspondence; maintains a current register of all members; issues notices of meetings in consultation with the President.

The Financial Secretary

Collects all dues, levies, and funds payable to the Association; issues receipts and keeps accurate financial records; prepares financial reports for the Congress.

The Treasurer

Custodian of all funds and properties; maintains the Association's bank account(s) jointly with the Financial Secretary and President; makes disbursements as authorised by the Executive Council.

The Director of Academics

Responsible for academic programmes, workshops, seminars, and tutorial sessions; liaises with the Tutorial Coordinator; represents the Association in academic forums within the University.

The Tutorial Coordinator

Plans, coordinates, and oversees all tutorial sessions and study groups; works with the Director of Academics to identify academic challenges and develop solutions; maintains records of tutorial attendance and outcomes.

The Director of Socials and Welfare

Responsible for the social welfare, counselling, and support of members; organises social events, cultural programmes, and welfare initiatives; monitors member well-being and reports concerns to the Executive Council.

The Director of Public Relations

Official spokesperson and media liaison; manages public communications, press releases, and promotional materials; works with the Social Media and IT Officer on the Association's online presence.

The Social Media and IT Officer

Manages and maintains all social media platforms, websites, and digital communications; documents events through photography and videography and manages the digital archive.

Article VII

Elections

Section 1 — General Provisions

Elections to all offices shall be free, fair, and credible, conducted by an Electoral Committee appointed by the Congress at least four (4) weeks before the election date. Voting shall be by secret ballot. General elections are held once per academic session, at a date determined by the Congress.

Section 2 — Eligibility to Contest

A candidate contesting for any executive office must:

  1. Be an ordinary member in good standing with no outstanding dues.
  2. Have a minimum CGPA of 2.0 on a 5-point scale, or its equivalent.
  3. Not be on academic probation, suspension, or rustication at the time of the election.
  4. Have served in the Association for at least one semester prior to the election (applicable to offices above class representative).
  5. Be duly nominated and seconded by registered members of the Association.
  6. Not hold more than one executive office simultaneously.

Section 3 — Tenure of Office

All executive officers serve a tenure of one (1) academic session, with a maximum of two (2) consecutive terms in the same office. A tenure begins at inauguration and ends on the day of a successor's inauguration.

Section 4 — Vacancy in Office

A vacancy is declared upon death, resignation, incapacitation, expulsion, or impeachment of an officer. If the presidency becomes vacant, the Vice President assumes the role for the remainder of the tenure. Other vacancies are filled by by-election or Executive Council appointment, subject to Congress ratification.

Article VIII

Impeachment and Removal from Office

  1. Any executive officer may be removed from office for gross misconduct, gross dereliction of duty, financial impropriety, or conduct unbecoming of an officer.
  2. Impeachment proceedings are initiated by a written petition signed by at least one-third (1/3) of the registered membership and submitted to the Secretary General.
  3. Upon receipt of a valid petition, the Congress shall be convened within fourteen (14) days to hear the matter.
  4. The officer subject to impeachment shall be given not less than seven (7) days' notice and a fair opportunity to defend themselves before the Congress.
  5. Removal from office requires a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Congress.
  6. An officer removed from office may appeal the decision to the Disciplinary Committee within seven (7) days of the decision.

Article IX

Standing Committees

The Executive Council shall establish Standing Committees to support the work of the Association, including:

  • The Academic Committee — chaired by the Director of Academics
  • The Finance and Audit Committee — chaired by the Treasurer
  • The Electoral Committee — appointed by the Congress before each election
  • The Disciplinary Committee — chaired by the Vice President
  • The Social and Welfare Committee — chaired by the Director of Socials and Welfare
  • The Public Relations and Media Committee — chaired by the Director of Public Relations

Additional ad hoc committees may be established by the Executive Council as needed. Each committee shall include at least three (3) ordinary members appointed by the President with Executive Council approval, and shall report its activities to the Executive Council at regular intervals.

Article X

Finances

Section 1 — Sources of Revenue

The funds of the Association shall be derived from: membership dues and registration fees approved by the Congress; levies and assessments imposed by the Congress; donations, grants, and subventions; proceeds from fundraising events and programmes; and any other lawful source approved by the Congress.

Section 2 — Financial Management

  1. All funds shall be deposited into a designated bank account in the name of the Association within seventy-two (72) hours of receipt.
  2. Withdrawals require the joint signatures of the President, Financial Secretary, and Treasurer.
  3. No expenditure exceeding five thousand naira (N5,000) shall be made without prior Executive Council approval. Expenditures above twenty thousand naira (N20,000) require Congress approval.
  4. Financial records shall be audited at the end of each academic session by the Finance and Audit Committee, with the report presented to the Congress.
  5. No officer or member shall use Association funds for personal benefit — any breach constitutes gross financial misconduct.

Article XI

Meetings

The Association shall hold: Congress (General Assembly) — at least once per semester; Executive Council meetings — at least once per month; Senate meetings — at least once per semester; and Committee meetings — as required.

Notice of Congress meetings shall be given at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance (emergency meetings may be convened with shorter notice). Quorum for Congress meetings is fifty percent (50%) of registered members; quorum for Executive Council meetings is a simple majority of officers. Decisions at all meetings are by simple majority except where this Constitution specifies a higher threshold. Minutes of all meetings are recorded by the Secretary General and confirmed at the next meeting.

Article XII

Programmes and Activities

The Association shall organise the following annual and recurrent programmes:

  • New Student Orientation Programme — welcoming and inducting 100-Level students and those transitioning to 200 Level
  • Tutorial and Academic Sessions — coordinated by the Tutorial Coordinator and Director of Academics
  • Nutrition Week/Symposium — an annual awareness event open to the University community
  • Community Outreach and Health Education Campaigns — at least once per session
  • Sports and Cultural Day — promoting fellowship and recreation among members
  • End-of-Session Dinner and Award Night — celebrating excellence and service

The Executive Council may propose additional programmes for Congress approval. All programmes shall be properly planned, budgeted, and reported upon completion.

Article XIII

Disciplinary Procedure

  1. Any complaint of misconduct against a member or officer shall be submitted in writing to the Secretary General.
  2. The Disciplinary Committee shall investigate the complaint within fourteen (14) days.
  3. The accused member or officer shall be notified of the complaint and given an opportunity to respond and be heard.
  4. The Disciplinary Committee shall submit its findings and recommendations to the Executive Council or Congress as appropriate.
  5. Available sanctions include: written warning, suspension from activities for a specified period, removal from office, and expulsion from the Association.
  6. A member or officer aggrieved by a disciplinary decision may appeal to the Congress within seven (7) days. The Congress's decision is final at the chapter level.

Article XIV

Relationship with the National Body and the University

  1. The NADSAN FUGUS Chapter shall remain a loyal and active chapter of NADSAN at the national level and shall comply with the national constitution and directives in all matters of national policy.
  2. The Association shall be represented at national NADSAN congresses and events by the President or a duly delegated officer.
  3. The Association shall work in cooperation with the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, the Faculty, and the University administration in the best interest of students.
  4. The Association shall not engage in any activity contrary to the laws and regulations of Federal University Gusau.

Article XV

Amendment of the Constitution

  1. This Constitution may be amended by a proposal presented in writing to the Secretary General and circulated to all members at least fourteen (14) days before a Congress meeting.
  2. Amendments shall be adopted by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of members present at a Congress meeting where quorum is established.
  3. No amendment shall be made that is contrary to the national constitution of NADSAN or the laws of Federal University Gusau.
  4. Amendments take effect immediately upon adoption unless the Congress specifies otherwise.

Article XVI

Dissolution

The Association may only be dissolved by a resolution supported by three-fourths (3/4) of all registered members at a specially convened Congress. In the event of dissolution, the assets and funds of the Association shall not be shared among members but shall be donated to the Department of Human Nutrition and Dietetics, FUGUS, or a charitable cause as determined by the Congress.

Article XVII

General Provisions

  1. The Association shall be non-partisan in all political matters and shall not affiliate with any political party.
  2. The Association shall be non-discriminatory, welcoming members regardless of gender, ethnicity, religion, or disability.
  3. All official communications of the Association shall be in English.
  4. This Constitution shall be the supreme law of the NADSAN FUGUS Chapter. Any regulation, policy, or decision inconsistent with this Constitution shall be void to the extent of the inconsistency.
  5. The interpretation of this Constitution in any dispute shall be the responsibility of the Executive Council, subject to review by the Congress.

Article XVIII

Transitional Provisions

  1. This Constitution shall come into force upon adoption by the Congress of the NADSAN FUGUS Chapter.
  2. The first Executive Council constituted under this Constitution shall serve for a period determined by the Congress pending regular elections.
  3. Any matter not specifically provided for in this Constitution shall be dealt with by the Executive Council, subject to ratification by the Congress at its next meeting.

Attestation
